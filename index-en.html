Our Inspection Process

We follow a standardized methodology aligned with port regulations and ROM recommendations in four key phases.

  1. 01

    Planning

    Reviewing drawings, historical inspection logs, and defining scope based on asset type and risk profile.

  2. 02

    Field Inspection

    High-resolution drone flights over the entire above-water structure, collecting georeferenced photos and video.

  3. 03

    Damage Assessment

    Classifying defects by severity and urgency, evaluating expected degradation and operational risk.

  4. 04

    Report & Action Plan

    Delivering a technical report with prioritized maintenance actions and indicative repair budgets.

Damage Severity Classification

Defects are categorized by severity to help managers prioritize maintenance spending.

Good

No significant defects. Standard preventive maintenance schedule.

Minor

Superficial or cosmetic wear. Structural capacity unaffected.

Monitor

Defect requiring tracking during upcoming periodic inspections.

Urgent

Structural or safety risk. Requires immediate corrective intervention.
